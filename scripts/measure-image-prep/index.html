Picture preparation measurement

Each input set becomes one image-to-pptx deck per screen size, made by the tool's engine entry (buildPptx in src/lib/pptx/assemblePptx.ts, as checked out) with the browser's shared preparation (sizedImagePreparation in src/lib/image/slideImage.ts), on a 16:9 slide with a white background, so its output check runs too. The preparation is only observed, not replaced: each prepared picture is recorded as it passes to the deck.

  • Inputs (spec #147's set): the JPG and PNG files in tests/fixtures and its subfolders; its HEIC files after the tool's own conversion (normalizeImageFile); and the first pages of its PDFs, drawn by pdf-to-image's engine (pdfToImages) at its defaults, 150 DPI, as JPG (its default) and as PNG. REAL_FIXTURES_DIR overrides the folder.
  • bytes: the prepared pictures' bytes, summed per set; the background is a colour, so it adds none. input is what the tool was given (a HEIC's converted JPEG, a PDF page's picture).
  • ΔE: CIEDE2000 between the picture drawn at its prepared size, before encoding (the same downscaleImage the preparation draws with), and the JPEG the deck receives, decoded; mean and 95th percentile over every second pixel in each direction. Only for pictures turned from PNG into JPEG.

Query ?sets=jpg,heic,png,pdf-jpg,pdf-png&screens=full-hd,4k&pdfs=<names>|all&pages=6 picks what to run (default: all sets, both screens, spec #147's five PDFs, 6 pages).
