ppt-compress re-encode ratios

Each image in the real-file decks and each loose JPEG/PNG (deduplicated) is re-encoded the way the engine does it, keeping the original when the candidate is no smaller. A ratio is the bytes left over the bytes going in.

  • fresh → P: the image as found, re-encoded at preset P. JPEGs are grouped by the quality they were saved at.
  • W → P: the image after the engine compressed it at weaker preset W, re-run at P. Skipped when W left the image unchanged, or (PNG) when W wrote it indexed, since both already count as compressed.
