ppt-compress re-encode ratios

Each image is re-encoded the way the engine does it, keeping the original when the candidate is no smaller. A ratio is the bytes left over the bytes going in; model is what the estimator (src/lib/ppt/pptCompressPlan.ts, as checked out) expects.

  • Fresh JPEG curve: lossless originals (PNGs under tests/lossless, and the HEIC fixtures) saved by this browser as fresh JPEGs at each quality, then re-encoded at each preset.
  • Real-file images: each image in the real-file decks and each loose JPEG/PNG (deduplicated). fresh → P is the image as found, re-encoded at preset P; W → P is the image after the engine compressed it at weaker preset W, re-run at P, skipped when W left it unchanged or (PNG) wrote it indexed, since both already count as compressed.
  • Decks: each real deck's estimate against the size its re-encoded images give, fresh and re-run (a deck compressed at W, then at P). The size ignores the zip's own deflate of the images, which is about 1%. HEIC sets are hold-out "decks" of the HEIC photos at one quality.

Query ?parts=curve,heic,png,fixtures picks what to run (default all). png re-encodes the lossless originals as fresh PNGs, as found and as this browser saves them.
