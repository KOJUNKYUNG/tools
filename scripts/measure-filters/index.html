ppt-background filter measurement

Each photo is cut to the largest 16:9 frame at its centre and prepared the way ppt-background applies it (slideImagePreparer in src/lib/image/slideImage.ts, with the filter set's step from src/lib/image/filterCanvas.ts, as checked out): fitted inside 4K, filtered, then saved as JPEG (q0.85, 4:2:0) or PNG by the existing format rule. Per filter setting:

  • ΔE: CIEDE2000 between the filtered picture before the final encode and the file the deck receives, decoded; mean and 95th percentile over every second pixel in each direction. It is the loss the encode adds, not the filter's own change, summarised over the results saved as JPEG (jpeg counts them; a PNG is lossless). none is the unfiltered result's. Pictures whose transparency is used stay PNG whatever the filters, so they are summarised apart.
  • size: the filtered file's bytes over the unfiltered result's for the same photo (a whole JPEG that fits 4K goes in unchanged, so its unfiltered result is the photo itself).
  • 4K time: the filter step alone, on photos whose crop fits to 3840 × 2160; a Paper filter's includes reading the picture, mounting Paper (a new WebGL context) and drawing. prepare is the whole preparation (cut, fit, filter, both encodes).
  • Solid colours: a colour made into a 4K picture, as the tool does for a filtered solid colour (its unfiltered result is a fill, so sizes are absolute).

Settings come from the filter set's own ranges (filtersFor): blur, veil and grain default to 0 (off), so each is measured at half its range and at its maximum; the veil both ways. A Paper filter's default strength is its maximum, 100%. Photos: the site's own gallery pictures (public/gallery, the tool's most common input), the loose JPEG/PNG files in tests/fixtures and its subfolders, the pictures in its decks whose long side is at least 1280 px, and the high-resolution set in tests/hires, deduplicated by bytes. Every picture is cut for a 16:9 slide, the default slide shape. Query ?sets=gallery,loose,decks,hires,solids&limit=N picks what to run (default all, no limit).
